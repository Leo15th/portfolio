A Little About Me

I'm Phyo Wai Aung, a frontend developer based in Thailand, specializing in websites for restaurants, cafés, and small shops. I build fast, clean, mobile-friendly sites that help local businesses look as good online as they do in person.

I believe good communication is just as important as good code. I keep clients updated throughout the project, respond quickly, and deliver on time — no surprises, no disappearing acts.

When I'm not building websites, I enjoy solving problems and learning new things. My goal is to grow alongside the businesses I work with — starting small, building trust, and delivering real results
